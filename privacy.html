Privacy Policy

Effective date: 8 November 2026 · Applies to: Cube Breaker: Pet Rescue, Boba Mix and other games published by Function Games
Türkçe metin için aşağıya bakın.

1. Who we are

Function Games is the game studio brand of Ali Özdemir, Türkiye ("Function Games", "we", "us"). We are the data controller for the processing described in this policy. Contact: aliozdemirbusiness@icloud.com.

2. The short version

  • You do not need an account to play. We do not ask for your name, e-mail, phone number or location.
  • Your game progress (levels, coins, boosters, settings) is stored only on your device.
  • The game is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. Google may collect device and usage data to show, measure and limit ads, as explained below.
  • On iOS, personalised ads based on tracking are only shown if you allow it in Apple's "Allow tracking" prompt. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings.
  • Some games (such as Boba Mix) use Google Analytics for Firebase to count anonymous gameplay events (for example levels started and won) so we can improve the game. It is not used to identify you or for advertising.

4. Advertising partners

We use Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC) to display ads. Google acts as an independent controller for its own purposes. Learn how Google uses information from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites, and Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. Ad partners that Google works with may also receive data as described in Google's policies.

Your choices:

  • iOS tracking: Choose "Ask App Not to Track" in the prompt, or go to Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Personalised ads (EEA, UK, Switzerland): where required, a consent message powered by Google is shown; you can change your choice at any time from the game's Settings → "Privacy choices".
  • Ad personalisation: Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising and adssettings.google.com.
  • No Ads purchase: if offered, removes interstitial ads; you can still choose to watch rewarded videos.

5. Sharing

We share data only with: Google AdMob and its ad partners (advertising, as above); Google (Google Analytics for Firebase, as our service provider for gameplay analytics); Apple (App Store distribution, TestFlight, payments and diagnostics); and authorities when required by law. We do not share data with other third parties.

6. International transfers

Google and Apple may process data in countries outside Türkiye and the EEA, including the United States. Such transfers rely on the providers' safeguards, such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses and the EU–US Data Privacy Framework, and — for transfers from Türkiye — on the mechanisms of KVKK Article 9, including your explicit consent where required.

7. Retention

Game progress stays on your device until you delete the app. Support e-mails are kept for up to 2 years. Gameplay analytics data is kept for up to 14 months. Advertising data is retained by Google according to its own retention policies. Apple retains purchase and diagnostic data under its policies.

8. Children

Our games are designed for a general audience and are not directed at children under 13 (or under the age of digital consent in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, KVKK Article 11, or US state laws such as the CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to: access, correct or delete your data; object to or restrict processing; withdraw consent at any time; data portability; learn whether your data is transferred abroad and to whom; and complain to a supervisory authority (in Türkiye: the Personal Data Protection Authority, kvkk.gov.tr). Because we do not keep accounts, most data exists only on your device (delete the app to erase it) or with Google (use the choices in section 4). To exercise any right, e-mail us; we answer within 30 days.

California residents: we do not "sell" personal information for money. Sharing of identifiers with ad partners for cross-context behavioural advertising only happens if you allow tracking; you can opt out at any time as described in section 4.

10. Security

We keep data to the minimum needed and rely on the security measures of Apple and Google. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above shows the latest version; material changes will be announced in the game.

12. Contact

Function Games – Ali Özdemir, Türkiye · aliozdemirbusiness@icloud.com
